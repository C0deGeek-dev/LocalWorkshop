The PolyForm Noncommercial License in [LICENSE](LICENSE) applies only to

LocalWorkshop does not relicense model weights, datasets, calibration sources,
converters, quantizers, or other upstream tools. Their names, revisions,
licenses, source URLs, hashes, and roles are recorded in `scripts/config.ps1`,

Produced weights inherit all applicable upstream terms. Review the complete
provenance manifest and each upstream license before use, deployment, or
redistribution. The default posture remains local use only; publishing weights
requires a separate explicit decision.
